        StickTD — Stick Tower Defense

        StickTD is a free browser tower defense game in development, playable on desktop and mobile without a download or account.

        Build a squad of stickmen, train their stats, and equip what the enemies drop. Choose positions that keep enemies in range. Strengthen the squad or spend resources on new ground.

        Terramorphing extends both ends of the path. Enemies that pass the finish remain on the map and can attack defenders; clear them to earn the wave-end expansion. Fight through 100 waves, then continue into endless play.

        Choose a defender in Build, place it on grass, then select Start Wave. Save a run to a file to continue later.

        🎁 Item Guide

        Defeated enemies sometimes leave an item on the road. Equipment shines with a rarity-based glow; coins and gold bags shine gold. Click a landed coin to collect its player gold, or click a Gold Bag to collect its listed amount.

        Equip an item by dragging it onto any stickman. Each item adds to Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence or Armor.

        Every stickman carries up to six items. A stickman with all six slots filled is promoted to Hero.

        Select a stickman and open its nameplate to see the items it carries. Drag an item out to hand it to another stickman.

        Some enemies leave a signature item of their own, and bosses always leave something special.

        ❓ How Everything Works

        ⭐ Training and Promotion

        Each tower has a promotion Rank and a separate Training level. Gold promotions raise Rank; they do not replace the Training XP bar. Kills, killstreaks, promotions, and surviving a round can grant Training XP. Each completed 100 XP bar rolls two lots of 1–3 spendable stat points, limited by remaining capacity. Promotions grant both automatic stat rolls and extra points for you to spend.

        💪 Stats

        STR: grows health, weight and body size. Warrior classes also use it for direct damage.
        DEX: improves attack speed, accuracy and critical chance. Archer classes also use it for direct damage.
        INT: grows reach toward each class's range cap and increases critical damage. Mage classes also use it for direct damage.

        Damage grows only from the class's preferred stat. Speed and critical bonuses have diminishing returns; equipment, class abilities and active buffs also affect the final numbers. Tap the DPS number for your selected unit's calculation.

        Train up to 500 points in one stat and 1,000 combined. Equipment and passive bonuses are separate from those trained-point limits.

        ⚔️ Archetypes

        Every fighting class favors one stat 25% more effective than the others: Warriors (Swordsman and the classes it unlocks) favor STR, Archer-style classes favor DEX, Mages favor INT.

        🔓 Unlocking new towers

        A tower never transforms into a new class — it stays exactly what it is forever and just keeps growing its own stats. What DOES happen: pushing a stat far enough locks in something about that tower, permanently, and pushing far enough again unlocks a different, better tower as directly buildable from the Build menu from then on, for the rest of the current game. The tower that earned the unlock stays exactly what it was.

        Exactly which stat leads where, and how far it takes, isn't spelled out here on purpose — part of the game is noticing the pattern yourself as towers change and new things unlock. Every locked slot in the Build menu shows the tower waiting to be found, with a hint at what to try next rather than an exact formula. Tap the ? on any locked row to see its hint.

        🎒 Items & Heroes

        Buy class-specific gear from the Shop and equip it from a tower's inspect panel. 6 inventory slots per tower. Fill all 6 with equipment to awaken a Hero with a permanent bonus to every stat; consumables do not count toward that equipment requirement.

        ✒️ Naming & Mastery

        At 500 combined trained stats, you can name your stickman. At 1,000, it earns a trophy and further XP converts to gold at 25 XP per coin.

        🔥 Status Effects

        Burn: ticks 4% max HP damage every 0.5s. Poison: flat damage every 0.6s, strength set by the Blowdart that applied it. Slow/Stun: from Mage and Hammerman. Shield: absorbs a set amount of damage before HP is touched, regenerating over time when not hit.

        ⚡ Kill Streaks

        The same stickman must keep landing kills within 1.2 seconds; a gap or another stickman taking the next kill resets the streak. Milestones run from Killing Spree at 100 kills to Ascended at 1,000 and grant a brief Bloodlust effect.

        💀 Downed Towers

        If a tower's HP hits 0, it's overrun and locked out of action for the next 2 full waves before recovering.

        🚧 How Barricades Work

        Barricades don't attack, don't earn EXP, don't level up, and can't unlock anything — they're a tough obstacle, not a fighter. Bought directly from Build (costs wood + stone, not gold, or free using a banked free-barricade charge) — tap Barricade in the Build menu, then tap a valid path tile to place it. Once placed, it physically blocks enemies from passing. It can still be picked back up (📦 Store) and re-placed or moved for free afterward, since it's already paid for.

        A Barricade's HP is its literal block-strength (shown as a fraction, e.g. 7/10) — it loses at most 1 HP every 10 seconds, with the first point requiring a full contact interval. All attackers share that limit, so a Barricade can hold a chokepoint before breaking.

        Each accepted expansion extends both ends of the existing path and reveals surrounding grass. Existing towers stay in place; blocked expansion does not spend your expansion payment. Extra expansions wait for the next round.
