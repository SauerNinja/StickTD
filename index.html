❓ How Everything Works

⭐ Leveling

Every tower has its own EXP level, up to 99 — separate from its gold-bought upgrade tier. Kills grant a little EXP, killstreaks grant a burst of it, upgrading with gold grants some too, and simply surviving to the end of a round grants EXP to every active tower. Each level-up hands out exactly 1 stat point — small and frequent rather than big lump sums.

💪 Stats

STR: +3% max HP per point for everyone, plus +6% damage per point — but only for Warrior-archetype towers.
DEX: +8% attack speed, accuracy, and 🍀 Luck (bonus gold per kill) per point for everyone, plus +6% damage per point — but only for Archer-style towers.
INT: +6 range per point for everyone, plus +6% damage per point — but only for Mage-archetype towers.
Dota-style: each class only gets bonus damage from its own preferred stat, never from the other two. Every 5 points invested, the next 5 are worth progressively less — heavy investment in one stat is never worthless, just increasingly inefficient. Points come from leveling up (see above), plus a little randomly whenever you spend gold to upgrade a tower's tier.

⚔️ Archetypes

Every fighting class favors one stat 25% more effective than the others: Warriors (Swordsman and its evolutions) favor STR, Archer-style classes favor DEX, Mages favor INT.

✨ Evolution

Only Swordsman, Archer, and Mage are built directly. Every other class is reached by investing 10+ points into a specific stat — a one-way transformation that keeps your accumulated stats.

⚔️ Swordsman → STR 10 → 🔨 Hammerman
⚔️ Swordsman → DEX 10 → 🪓 Axeman
⚔️ Swordsman → INT 10 → 🔱 Spearman
🏹 Archer → STR 10 → 🔫 Gatling
🏹 Archer → DEX 10 → 🎯 Blowdart
🎯 Blowdart → DEX 25 → 🔫 Dual Squirt Gun
🏹 Archer → INT 10 → 💣 Bomber
💣 Bomber → INT 25 → 🔫 Gunalinder
🔫 Gunalinder → INT 40 → 🎯 Sniper
🔮 Mage → INT 10 → ✝️ Cleric

🎒 Items & Heroes

Buy class-specific gear from the Shop and equip it from a tower's inspect panel. 6 slots per tower — fill all 6 to awaken it as a Hero with a permanent bonus to every stat.

🏆 100 Total Stats

A tower that reaches 100 combined STR+DEX+INT becomes Legendary — you'll name it, and it permanently gains +10% size, +5% max HP, and +5% damage reduction.

🔥 Status Effects

Burn: ticks 4% max HP damage every 0.5s. Poison: flat damage every 0.6s, strength set by the Blowdart that applied it. Slow/Stun: from Mage and Hammerman. Shield: absorbs a set amount of damage before HP is touched, regenerating over time when not hit.

⚡ Kill Streaks

Kills within 1.2 seconds of each other build a streak — the same tower has to keep landing hits fast, or it resets. Every 10 kills in a row unlocks a tier, from Killing Spree up to Ascended at 100, each granting a bonus stat point.

💀 Downed Towers

If a tower's HP hits 0, it's overrun and locked out of action for the next 2 full waves before recovering.

🚧 How Barricades Work

Barricades don't attack, don't earn EXP, don't level up, and can't evolve — they're a cheap, tough obstacle, not a fighter. Place one directly on the path and it physically blocks enemies from passing.

A Barricade's HP is its literal block-strength (shown as a fraction, e.g. 7/10) — it only loses HP while something is actively pressed against it, at most 1 point every 2 seconds, so a single Barricade can hold a chokepoint for a long time before breaking.

The map starts small and expands ring by ring as you clear waves — at most one expansion per round, plus a free one every few waves. The path is regenerated (and re-checked against your existing towers) every time the map expands.
